Nordic Walking United (NWU) is built on kindness, community, and the joy of moving together in nature.

To keep every walk, workshop, and gathering safe, respectful, and uplifting, all Organizers, Hosts, Members, Participants, and Guests agree to follow this Code of Conduct and organizer guidelines.

This shared commitment ensures that NWU events remain welcoming, inclusive, and enjoyable for everyone — from first-time walkers to experienced hosts.

{% trans "Organizer Basics" %}

Most NWU walks are designed to be enjoyable and accessible for a wide range of people.

  • Typical walks last 30–90 minutes, including warm-up and cool-down.
  • Longer walks are fine as long as they match your group’s ability level.

A sweep walks at the end of the group and keeps everyone together.

  • Recommended for groups with more than 8 people.
  • Optional for small groups — but always helpful.

Safety always comes first. You may:

  • cancel the event,
  • postpone,
  • switch to a safer nearby route.

Always update participants on the NWU event page.

{% trans "Safety, Respect & Photos" %}

You must honor their request — always.

  • Even with signed consent, some may opt out for that event.
  • Ask before the walk: "Does anyone prefer not to be in photos today?"
  • If someone objects → do not include them.
  • When unsure, ask before posting photos.

  • Stop the group.
  • Call emergency services if needed.
  • Stay with the injured/unwell person until help arrives.
  • Never send the rest of the group back without a leader.

Afterward, notify NWU via the CONTACT US page.

You may:

  • give a calm verbal warning,
  • document the incident,
  • report it to NWU via CONTACT US.

If safety is threatened, you may ask the person to leave.

{% trans "Group Dynamics & Growth" %}

The goal is for everyone to feel welcome and supported.

  • Keep the pace conversational.
  • Ask faster walkers to regroup regularly.
  • Use a sweep to support slower walkers.
  • Choose routes with natural regroup points.

  • Post friendly follow-up summaries.
  • Share photos (only with consent).
  • Engage warmly in comments.
  • Encourage participants to invite friends.
  • Be consistent — warmth + reliability = growth.

{% trans "Policies & Fees" %}

Donations are allowed only if they are clearly stated as optional.

  • thank participants,
  • share a warm summary,
  • add photos (with permission),
  • mention the next walk.

{% trans "Help & Support" %}

Organizers are never alone.

  • Contact NWU leadership anytime via CONTACT US.
  • We help with safety, planning, group dynamics, and more.
  • If you're unsure how to handle a situation, reach out — we are here to support you.
