Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

GENERAL QUESTIONS

Nordic Walking United is a global, community-driven initiative that brings people together through Nordic Walking, movement, nature, wellness, and connection.

Everyone!
All ages, all fitness levels, beginners and experienced walkers alike are welcome. You don’t need prior experience — just curiosity and comfortable shoes.

Poles are recommended, but not always required.

Some groups are happy to provide poles for beginners and can help you get started. Please check the event details, or feel free to ask the organizer by registering for the event and posting your question in the comments.

Yes, NWU events are free to join.

Some events may include optional donations to support your local Nordic Walking group and community.

NWU walks happen all over the world — in parks, trails, beaches, city paths, and nature preserves.

Visit the Events page to see upcoming walks by location and date. You can also browse the Nordic Walking Groups section or MAP section to discover local and international NWU communities.

FINDING WALKS & GROUPS

Visit the Events page or explore the Map to discover upcoming walks near you.

Browse Nordic Walking Groups to find welcoming communities around the world.

You can join a walk while traveling, or start your own group using the “Organize a Nordic Walking Group” button on our homepage.

ORGANIZING A GROUP

Anyone who loves people, enjoys being active, and brings others together through Nordic Walking — while supporting NWU values of inclusivity, respect, and community.

Simply click the “Organize a Nordic Walking Group” button on the main page and follow the steps.

Once approved, your group and events will appear on the NWU website for walkers around the world to find.

Yes!
We provide step-by-step TUTORIAL and GUIDELINES in the RESOURCES tab, including:
  • How to create events
  • How to manage your group
  • Best practices for welcoming participants
  • Safety and communication tips
  • NWU guidelines

Absolutely. NWU is a global community.

Groups are welcome in any country, city, or region, as long as they follow NWU principles and guidelines.

WEBSITE & ACCOUNT

Yes. A quick registration helps our event organizers prepare and keep everyone safe. Creating an account allows you to register for events, stay informed, and easily connect with the NWU community.

Please visit the Resources tab for tutorials and instructions.

If you still have questions, use the Contact Us page — we read every message.

VALUES & COMMUNITY

Nordic Walking United is built on:
  • Movement with joy, not pressure
  • Nature as a shared space to care for
  • Kindness, inclusivity, and respect
  • Community before competition
  • A focus on the wellbeing of every NWU member
We walk together — across borders, languages, and generations.

The best support is walking with us, inviting others, organizing groups, and spreading kindness and connection.

Optional donations are always welcome and help keep NWU operational, accessible, and growing — but participation is what truly matters.
