{% trans "Code of Conduct for Organizers, Hosts & Members" %}

Nordic Walking United (NWU) is built on kindness, community, and the joy of moving together in nature.

To keep every walk, workshop, and gathering safe, respectful, and uplifting, all Organizers, Hosts, Members, Participants, and Guests agree to follow this Code of Conduct.

This shared commitment ensures that NWU events remain welcoming, inclusive, and enjoyable for everyone — from first-time walkers to experienced hosts.

A. Expectations for All NWU Participants & Members

1. Be Kind, Respectful & Inclusive

Everyone is expected to:

  • Treat others with courtesy, patience, and kindness.
  • Welcome new walkers warmly.
  • Respect differences in age, ability, pace, and background.
  • Use inclusive, supportive language.

NWU is a place where everyone belongs.

2. Honor Personal Boundaries

  • Respect personal space and comfort levels.
  • Ask before offering physical assistance.
  • Avoid comments about body, appearance, or personal matters.
  • Be mindful with humor and interactions.

We walk together — but always with respect.

3. Privacy & Photos

  • Participants sign a Photo/Media Consent form when joining NWU.
  • However, if someone requests NOT to be photographed or filmed at a specific event, that request must always be honored.
  • When in doubt, ask before posting photos or videos.

Everyone has the right to control how they are represented.

4. Zero Tolerance for Harassment

NWU does not tolerate:

  • Harassment or bullying
  • Discrimination of any kind
  • Unwanted physical contact
  • Racist, sexist, or exclusionary language
  • Sexualized comments or behavior
  • Aggressive, intimidating, or disruptive actions

If you feel uncomfortable, speak to the event organizer immediately.

5. Walk Safely & Respect Nature

  • Be aware of terrain, weather, and hazards.
  • Inform the organizer if you feel unwell or need to slow down.
  • Respect trails, parks, wildlife, and public spaces.
  • Carry out any trash.

We move with gratitude toward nature and each other.

B. Expectations for NWU Organizers & Hosts

Organizers and hosts are the guiding heart of Nordic Walking United. You set the tone for each gathering and ensure every event is safe, inclusive, and uplifting. By hosting an event, you agree to uphold the following:

1. Creating a Welcoming Environment

Organizers should:

  • Greet participants warmly and help newcomers feel included.
  • Encourage camaraderie, not competition.
  • Celebrate diversity in age, ability, background, and experience.
  • Use supportive and inclusive language.

2. Respect & Professional Conduct

As a representative of NWU, you are expected to:

  • Treat all participants with respect and courtesy.
  • Avoid sarcasm, belittling remarks, or dismissive behavior.
  • Keep personal conflicts separate from events.
  • Refrain from vulgar, discriminatory, or inappropriate language.
  • Avoid polarizing political or religious discussions during events.

3. Privacy & Consent (Organizer Responsibilities)

Organizers must:

  • Follow the Photo/Media Consent guidelines.
  • Never post or share photos/videos of someone who has asked not to be photographed or filmed.
  • Respect real-time requests such as:
    • “Please don’t include me in photos today.”
    • “I prefer not to be filmed.”
  • Keep personal information confidential unless permission is given.

4. Safety & Risk Awareness

Organizers must:

  • Select routes suitable for the group’s ability level.
  • Explain potential hazards (roots, mud, ice, boardwalks, uneven terrain).
  • Ensure no one is left behind — check on slower walkers.
  • Stay until every participant has safely returned.
  • Keep a charged phone on hand when possible.
  • Encourage hydration, proper clothing, warm-ups, and healthy pacing.

5. Zero Tolerance for Harassment

If someone behaves in a way that threatens others’ comfort or safety, organizers may:

  • Issue a verbal warning
  • Ask the person to leave the event
  • Contact authorities if necessary
  • Report the incident to NWU leadership through the website

Organizers help maintain a respectful environment — not adjudicate or investigate.

6. Handling Concerns or Incidents

If a participant reports discomfort, disrespect, or unsafe behavior:

  1. Listen calmly and without judgment.
  2. Take their concern seriously.
  3. Do not share the issue publicly unless they request it.
  4. Take reasonable steps to stop or de-escalate the situation.
  5. Document what happened (time, date, details).
  6. Notify NWU leadership via the CONTACT US tab on the NWU website.

Your role is to support — not investigate or assign blame.

7. Representing NWU With Integrity

Organizers and hosts are encouraged to:

  • Lead by example with kindness and positivity.
  • Promote environmental stewardship and community service.
  • Uphold NWU values online and in person.
  • Keep NWU events free or donation-based unless otherwise approved.

C. Commitment to the Community

By participating in or hosting NWU events, you affirm that you will:

  • Follow this Code of Conduct.
  • Prioritize safety and well-being.
  • Support inclusivity, kindness, and respect.
  • Help NWU remain a welcoming community for all.

Together, we create the NWU spirit — joyful, healthy, and connected.
